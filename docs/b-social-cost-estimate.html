Agency Tier Comparison High vs Mid

🏢 High-End Agency

€420K – €680K

Premium Scandinavian/Western European firms (Netcompany, Trifork, Nodes, Reaktor-level)

  • €140–200/hour senior rates
  • 6–10 months, team of 8–10
  • 1 Project Manager (~€80–100K)
  • 1 UX/UI Designer (~€60–80K)
  • 2 Senior Frontend Devs (~€120–160K)
  • 1 Senior Backend Dev (~€60–80K)
  • 1 DevOps Engineer (~€30–40K)
  • 1 QA Engineer (~€40–60K)
  • Extensive planning phase (3–4 months)
  • Agency overhead: 30–50%
  • Changes = change requests = more cost
  • Custom design system, pixel-perfect
  • Full test coverage & code reviews
  • Dedicated account manager

🏗️ Mid-Range Agency

€180K – €320K

Competent mid-tier firms or smaller boutique studios (Eastern Europe, Iberia, or smaller Nordic shops)

  • €80–130/hour mixed rates
  • 5–8 months, team of 5–7
  • 1 PM / Tech Lead combo (~€40–60K)
  • 1 UX/UI Designer (~€30–50K)
  • 2 Mid-Senior Frontend Devs (~€60–100K)
  • 1 Backend Dev (~€30–50K)
  • Part-time QA (~€15–25K)
  • Shorter planning phase (1–2 months)
  • Less overhead: 15–25%
  • Template-based design, less custom
  • Functional testing, less code review
  • May use junior devs on some features
  • Good quality, but less polish

What this estimate covers

This is a from-scratch build estimate — the cost to recreate everything B-Social currently has if you walked into a high-end agency.

Includes: design, development, testing, PM, deployment, and data pipeline (10 edge functions, 7+ APIs).

NOT included: Ongoing maintenance (€2–5K/month), native mobile apps (+€150–300K), marketing, legal/GDPR audit (~€10–20K), future features.
